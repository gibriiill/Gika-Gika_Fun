### Project Description
Gika Gika Fun brings Malayalam proverbs to life. Nine — well, eleven — rowboats drift across an animated harbor, each one rowed by a stickman with your face on it. Click a boat and it takes you to its own mini interactive experience: a tiny game, animation, or simulation built entirely around a single Malayalam proverb, so you don't just read the saying, you get to live it out.

## Technical Details

### Technologies/Components Used
For Software:
- HTML5
- CSS3 (custom animations, gradients, keyframes — no CSS frameworks)
- Vanilla JavaScript (no frameworks or libraries)
- SVG (hand-built illustrations and animations)
- HTML5 Canvas (scratch-to-reveal and physics-based interactions)
- Web Audio API (synthesized sound effects — crow caws, elephant trumpets)
- Google Fonts (Noto Sans Malayalam, Baloo 2, Baloo Chettan 2, Manjari, Fraunces, Anek Malayalam, Inter)
- Deployed as a static site on Vercel

For Hardware:
- None — this is a purely web-based, front-end only project.

```bash

```

Or simply open `index.html` directly in a browser.

# Additional Demos
- The Harbor (`index.html`): nine animated rowboats sail across the screen; each carries a stickman rower and a banner naming its proverb, and links to its own page.
- Boat 1 — കാക്ക കുളിച്ചാൽ കൊക്കാവില്ല: turn on the shower, soap up the crow, and watch it transform into a random bird — everything except the one thing the proverb says it can never become.
- Boat 2 — എല്ലാത്തിനും അതിന്റേതായ സമയം ഉണ്ട് ദാസാ!: enter your name and get a random "fate ticket" with a comically specific date, time, and life event.
- Boat 3 — കാക്കയ്ക്കും തൻകുഞ്ഞ് പൊൻകുഞ്ഞ്: spot the crow's chick among a nest of colourful lookalikes.
- Boat 4 — ഇതിനു പിന്നിൽ വലിയ ഒരു കഥയുണ്ട്!: scratch away a scratch-card-style cover to reveal a short story about an everyday object.
- Boat 5 — കുടം പൊട്ടിയാൽ വെള്ളം പോവും: pull back a slingshot, aim using the live trajectory preview, and shatter the hanging pot.
- Boat 6 — അധികം ആയാൽ അമൃതും വിഷം: pour a cup, decide when to stop, and see whether moderation paid off.
- Boat 7 — ഇത്തിരി നേരം ഇരുന്നാൽ എന്താ, മല ഇടിഞ്ഞു വീഴുമോ?: sit in the chair and watch the mountain behind you literally answer the question.
- Boat 8 — മത്തൻ കുത്തിയാൽ കുമ്പളം മുളക്കില്ല: plant a seed, water it, and watch it grow into exactly what you planted.
- Boat 9 — പൂച്ചയ്ക്ക് പൊന്നുരുക്കി കൊടുത്താലും അത് മീൻ തന്നെ നോക്കും: melt gold in a furnace, place it beside a fish, and see which one the cat actually wants.
- Boat 10 — അടിച്ചാൽ ആനയും വീഴും!: time your tap on a moving elephant on a tightrope to knock it down.
- Boat 11 — അടുപ്പത്ത് വെച്ചത് കാക്ക കൊണ്ടുപോയി: cook an egg step by step on the stove, only for a crow to swoop in and steal the pan.
